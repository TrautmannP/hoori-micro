## The pattern

1. **Prepare remotely first** – read from other services *before* opening a transaction.
2. **Write locally and briefly** – a decorated `@Transactional` interface writes data and outbox
   together.
3. **Respond after commit** – the physical commit happens before response encoding.

## Rules

- Keep remote preparation **outside** short local transactions.
- Handles are owner-bound. Child tasks never inherit a handle; an explicitly started child
  transaction is independent.
- Same-owner `REQUIRED` nesting reuses the handle and marks it rollback-only on failure.
- `COMMITTED` and `UNKNOWN` outcomes are preserved – **never** reported as rollback or retried.
  A lost commit acknowledgement stays `UNKNOWN` and discards the connection without replay.

<Callout type="warn">
  No exactly-once guarantee, no outbox delivery and no distributed ACID semantics are implied.
</Callout>
